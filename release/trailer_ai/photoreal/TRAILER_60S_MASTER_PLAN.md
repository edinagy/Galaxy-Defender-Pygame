# GALAXY DEFENDER — 60-second cinematic trailer

## Creative direction

This is a photoreal live-action/VFX science-fiction trailer inspired by the game's story, not a representation of gameplay. Every spacecraft must have believable mass, manufactured materials, physical lighting, restrained energy effects and consistent geometry. Do not add a HUD or simulate a top-down game camera.

The eight AI video clips should be generated at 1080p, 16:9, 24 FPS, 8 seconds, with audio disabled. Trim them to the timings below during editing. Add narration, music and sound design only in the final edit.

## Original narrator direction

Original male bass-baritone narrator with an extremely deep, ancient and commanding presence. Slow measured cadence, calm authority, restrained emotion and precise consonants. Natural human performance with a very subtle metallic subharmonic resonance, close-mic cinematic recording and wide dynamic range. He should sound like a timeless guardian recounting the beginning of a war, never like an advertising announcer. Do not imitate or clone any existing actor or fictional character.
