# Galaxy Defender — draft Steam Store

## Short description

Enter enemy territory in a cinematic space campaign, then survive an escalating
endless war. Chain kills, graze hostile fire, defeat evolving Sovereigns and
climb the global leaderboard.

## About This Game

Galaxy Defender is a score-driven 2D space shooter built around one question:
how deep can you push into enemy territory before your five lives run out?

Fight through a cinematic opening campaign, then enter an endless sequence of
waves, space hazards and multi-phase boss battles. Every defeated Sovereign
opens a more dangerous stage. Enemies move faster, attacks intensify, special
events arrive sooner and score rewards grow with the risk.

### Features

- Endless score runs with escalating stages and global leaderboard support
- Kill Combo and Graze Chain systems that reward precision and aggressive play
- Six enemy classes, ten dynamic space events and an evolving three-phase boss
- Four weapon levels, shields, extra lives and a screen-clearing Energy Pulse
- Eight achievements, local progress saving and offline score queue
- Full keyboard and Xbox-style controller support
- Cinematic campaign with separate New Game and Continue flows
